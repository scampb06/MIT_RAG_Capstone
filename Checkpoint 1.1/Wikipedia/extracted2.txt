ALASKA CASUALTIES CONTEXT:
Casualties 139 deaths The 1964 Alaska earthquake , also known as the Great Alaska earthquake and Good Friday earthquake , occurred at 5:36 PM AKST on Good Friday , March 27, 1964. [ 2 ] Across south-central Alaska , ground fissures, collapsing structures, and tsunamis resulting from the earthquake caused about 139 deaths. [ 3 ] Lasting four minutes and thirty-eight seconds, the magnitude 9.2–9.3 megathrust earthquake remains the most powerful earthquake ever recorded in North America [ 2 ] [ 4 ] [ 5 ] and the second most powerful earthquake ever recorded in the world since modern seismography
